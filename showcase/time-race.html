What this shows, and what it does not

How to read it

  • This is a model. The only measured parts are the search times and the rule-check time. Everything a person does on either side is an assumption.
  • The MUTABIQ side still includes human time: entering the product, reviewing every finding against its cited article, and checking the form. MUTABIQ prepares the file. The applicant signs it and the authority decides.
  • The rule checks in the app are deterministic code written for this one trial case, on sample company and product data.
  • Search is not perfect. Two of the ten questions did not bring TRC text to the top, which is why every finding goes to a person for review.
  • Search times are from one machine with the model already loaded. Slower computers will take longer, but still seconds, not hours.

How to turn this into a measurement

  1. Give two or three people the DEMO-X1 sample documents and the seven official PDFs.
  2. Time each person answering the four questions by hand, and filling the Annex 1 form.
  3. Time the same people doing the same case in MUTABIQ, including their review of every finding.
  4. Put the times into the sliders above and say how many people took part.
  5. Better still, run it with real importers in a pilot. That is the number to quote.
